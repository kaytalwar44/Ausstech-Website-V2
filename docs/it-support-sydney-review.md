**No confirmed public physical address has been supplied.** No street address,
postcode, office location, coordinates, opening hours, ratings, certification,
Microsoft partner status, client counts or guaranteed response times are
invented. The Sydney suburbs in the copy describe possible team locations, not
claimed Auss-Tech office branches.

Google lists a physical business address as required for LocalBusiness rich
results. The requested service-area-only markup is structurally checked, but
Google local-business rich-result eligibility is **not** claimed. A verified
address approved for public use is needed before reassessing that eligibility.
FAQ rich-result eligibility is not promised.
